How to Back Up iPhone Live Photos Without Losing the Motion

2026-11-25 #Live Photos #iPhone #photo backup #HEIC

Live Photos capture the second and a half around the shutter — the laugh just before the smile, the wave that ends a goodbye. They're also the photos most likely to be quietly damaged by a backup. Here's why, and how to make sure yours keep moving.

What a Live Photo really is

A Live Photo isn't one file. It's a still image (usually HEIC) paired with a short video clip (MOV) recorded around the moment you pressed the shutter. The Photos app links the two and plays the clip when you press and hold.

If a backup copies only the image, you get a nice still photo — and lose the motion forever once the original is deleted from your phone.

Which methods keep the motion?

Method Keeps Live Photo motion?
Sending through most messaging apps Usually no (and compressed)
Emailing or uploading to social media Usually no
USB import to a computer Often as two separate files, sometimes the image only
iCloud Photos Yes
Lomorage home backup Yes — Live Photos are backed up with original photos and videos

Back up Live Photos to your own computer

  1. Install the free Lomorage desktop app on a Windows PC or Mac from the download section.
  2. Install LomoMobile on your iPhone and scan the setup code on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Allow access to the albums you want to back up and let the first backup finish with the app open.
  4. Turn on automatic backup (Wi-Fi-only, charging-only or nighttime).

If iCloud's "Optimize iPhone Storage" is on, the phone may only have previews. Download originals first, or back up in batches. See the full iPhone guide.

Check before you delete

Open a few backed-up Live Photos — new and old — and press and hold to see them move. Only then use Free Up Space or delete originals from your phone.

A note on edited Live Photos

On iOS, if a photo has been edited, Lomorage stores the edited version. If you need the pre-edit original as well, see the FAQ entry on edited files.
