iCloud Storage Full? 5 Fixes That Don't Require a Bigger Plan

2026-11-25 #iCloud #iPhone #photo backup #phone storage

The "iCloud Storage Is Full" notification usually comes with one suggested fix: upgrade. Before you sign up for another monthly charge, try these five fixes — most families can recover a lot of space, and keep their photos safer at the same time.

First, see what's using the space

On iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. You'll typically see:

  1. Photos — usually the largest by far.
  2. Backups — full device backups for every iPhone and iPad on the account, including old devices you no longer use.
  3. Messages — photos and videos in conversations.
  4. iCloud Drive and app data.

Fix 1: Delete backups of old devices

Under Backups, you may find a backup of a phone you sold years ago. Deleting old device backups is often the fastest win. Keep the backup of the phone you use now.

Fix 2: Exclude big apps from your device backup

In the current device's backup settings, turn off apps that don't need backing up — especially apps that re-download their content anyway. If Photos is already synced with iCloud Photos or backed up elsewhere, it doesn't need to be in the device backup too.

Fix 3: Clean up message attachments

Large videos sent in Messages count against iCloud. In Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Messages, review large attachments, or set messages to keep for one year instead of forever.

Fix 4: Remove the obvious photo clutter

Screenshots, screen recordings, burst shots and near-duplicate photos add up. Delete what you truly don't need — then remember that Recently Deleted keeps items for about 30 days before the space is released.

Fix 5: Move your photo library to a drive you own

This is the fix that actually stops the problem coming back. Instead of paying more each year as your library grows, keep your photos on a computer or NAS at home:

  1. Make sure your iPhone holds the originals (switch to Download and Keep Originals if you have the space, or back up in batches).
  2. Set up Lomorage on a Windows PC or Mac and install LomoMobile on your iPhone. Photos, videos and Live Photos back up over Wi-Fi as originals. Full walkthrough: back up iPhone photos without iCloud.
  3. Verify the backup — open a few photos and videos from the computer.
  4. Then decide: keep iCloud Photos for convenience with a home copy as protection, or turn it off and use the free space for device backups.
