Phone and web apps

Use LomoMobile to connect to your Lomorage library, back up new photos and revisit family memories.

Choose your phone

  • iPhone and iPad
  • Android

During first setup, keep the phone and Lomorage storage device on the same home network. A family member can also sign in by scanning a pairing QR code, avoiding the need to type a server address and password.

Use a browser

The web app lets you browse and upload from a computer. It also includes an Inbox for photos and videos shared by family members.

After connecting, try a small backup and confirm that the photos open before moving the whole library.
