Which computer do you use at home?

Your computer stores the photos. Open this page on that computer and make sure it has enough free disk space.

Choose your computer

  1. Open a command window

    Press Win + R, type cmd, and press Enter. You only need to copy the command below; no administrator password is needed.

  2. Copy the command, paste it, and press Enter

    powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://lomorage.com/windows/install.ps1 | iex"

  3. Wait for the setup page to open

    Installation starts Lomorage and opens its setup page. For a first setup, keep the QR code on screen to scan with the phone app next. Later, search for Lomorage to reopen it.

For Windows x64 computers. Stay connected to the internet during installation; there is no need to paste the command again.

Good questions

A little clarity, before you start.

Explore all FAQs →
Do I install it on my phone or my computer?+

For the first setup, prepare photo storage on a computer or NAS and install the phone app. If your household already has Lomorage set up and an account ready for you, just install the phone app and scan your sign-in code.

Do I have to do something every day?+

Enable automatic backup and choose Wi-Fi-only, charging-only or nighttime backups, plus the folders to include. Your computer or NAS must be on and reachable. Phone systems limit background activity, so keep the app open for a first or large backup and check completion.

Can I delete photos from my phone afterward?+

Free Up Space can remove large local files that have already been backed up. Confirm backup is complete and the originals open correctly before clearing space. Keep another independent copy of irreplaceable photos.

What if my computer breaks?+

Local storage is not a guarantee against loss. Drives can fail and files can be accidentally deleted. Regularly copy important photos to a separate drive and check that those files open correctly.

Does my computer need to be on to view photos?+

To access originals stored on your computer, it must be on and reachable. Offline browsing on your phone depends on what the app has cached; do not assume every original is available offline.
