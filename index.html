Keep Memories at Home, Give Privacy Back to Yourself

Lomorage is an open-source private photo vault designed for families. Instead of chasing feature bloat and complex updates, we focus on two core needs: saving your phone’s storage and serving as a rock-solid, original-quality data safe. No monthly cloud fees, 100% private.

Why Lomorage? Focus on Extreme Stability, Simplicity, and Security

Most open-source photo apps (like Immich) pursue feature bloat, leading to complex deployment barriers and frequent updates that break systems. Lomorage takes the opposite path, delivering a set-and-forget appliance-like stability to safeguard your digital assets.

Need 1: Save Phone Storage

Phone storage full, forced to delete photos daily? Lomorage creates a secure local “bottomless pit”. Once photos are backed up to your local server, safely clear originals on your phone to instantly reclaim tens of GB, saving you money on buying a bigger phone or subscribing to iCloud.

Need 2: Original Data Safe

Phones get lost, broken, or water-damaged. Memories shouldn’t vanish. Lomorage provides peace of mind with a local backup safe. You don’t need to check old photos daily, but you can rest assured: the moment you connect to your home Wi-Fi, original photos and 4K videos are secured on your hard drive.

Innovation: AI Photo Slimming

Afraid of clearing your phone? Our PC-side AI analyzes photos overnight (zero mobile battery drain): low-value screenshots are suggested for deletion; daily scenery photos are replaced with 50KB HD thumbnails; high-value family photos stay on the phone for offline immediate viewing.

Extreme Stability & Simplicity

We refuse bloated features like maps, complex UI, and frequent updates that break systems. Lomorage focuses on appliance-like stability. Run it on an old laptop, spare PC, or single-board computer, and enjoy a low-maintenance, set-and-forget backup environment.

Plug & Play, No Lock-In

Supports FAT32, NTFS, EXT, and other major file systems. Plug in your existing external hard drive and start backing up right away with no reformatting needed. Even if you stop using Lomorage, your photos remain organized and readable on any computer.

Independent Accounts, 100% Private

One server supports unlimited family accounts, each completely isolated. Your photos are private, transferred at high speeds only over your local network, with zero cloud tracking or analytics. Your memories belong only to you.
